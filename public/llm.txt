## Summary

Sai Likhith Kanuparthi is a Senior AI Infrastructure & Systems Engineer at Airbnb in San Francisco. He owns end-to-end architecture of BPI Virtual Analyst, a multi-model GenAI orchestration platform abstracting 30+ foundation models (AWS Bedrock, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, vLLM) behind FacadeDriver with routing, retry, fallback, and graceful degradation. The platform serves 55+ analysts across 4 partner engineering teams, processing 10K rows per run and 40MB uploads with PII-safe inference.

He has 8+ years of experience shipping production AI systems across LLM serving, Kafka streaming (4M req/min at Southwest Airlines), distributed observability (OpenTelemetry, Loki, Datadog, Grafana), backend APIs, cloud data pipelines, and full-stack applications. He is a published AI researcher with papers at SPE ADIPEC 2022 (SPE-210986-MS), IEEE Xplore, and Cambridge Scholars Publishing (2 book chapters, 2025).
